### ELEVATE 2025-Educate

Keynote: Beyond language specialists
主题演讲：超越语言专家

Last year we said goodbye to translators and hello to language specialists – have we moved beyond that?
去年，我们告别了翻译人员，向语言专家问好 - 我们是否已经超越了这一点？

This is us being a bit more concrete on where we see roles evolving. There will be a lot more focus on validation rather than translation. What we call language specialists now need to start being more comfortable with the notion of only signing pretranslated content off or reworking it if necessary in contrast to actually translating it. Answering the questions: Is the content factually correct? Is it usable (i.e. will users be able to cope in the target market)? Is it compliant with market regulations (e.g. for pharmaceutical or patent content)? Will it connect with its target audience? That concretely means a lot more focus on subject matter expertise beyond the pure language skills. It also means we need to be able to have a more diverse approach to language quality around those criteria and find a way to better articulate what good enough looks like. It also implies more focus on audiovisual work as content generators want to establish a deeper connection. And more focus on software localization and testing since content users will expect intuitive, native user experiences as they navigate an application. The single exception to that will be more focus on low resource languages, where AI capabilities struggle to this date. That said: Even here, progress in AI capabilities, quantum computing, etc. will break down that barrier faster than we think, so getting ready to invest more time into understanding how to validate areas like compliance, usability, human connection will even there be time well spent.
这是我们对角色演变的更具体一点。将更加关注验证而不是翻译。我们所谓的语言专家现在需要开始更适应只签署预翻译内容或在必要时对其进行返工的概念，而不是实际翻译它。回答问题：内容是否真实正确？它是否可用（即用户是否能够应对目标市场）？是否符合市场法规（例如药品或专利内容）？它会与目标受众建立联系吗？具体来说，这意味着在纯粹的语言技能之外，人们更加关注主题专业知识。这也意味着我们需要能够围绕这些标准采用更多样化的语言质量方法，并找到一种方法来更好地阐明“足够好”是什么样子。这也意味着更加关注视听工作，因为内容生成器希望建立更深层次的联系。并且更加关注软件本地化和测试，因为内容用户在浏览应用程序时将期望获得直观的原生用户体验。唯一的例外是更加关注资源匮乏的语言，而 AI 功能至今仍在努力。也就是说：即使在这里，AI 能力、量子计算等方面的进步也会比我们想象的更快地打破这一障碍，因此准备好投入更多时间来了解如何验证合规性、可用性、人际关系等领域，甚至会花费时间。

5 Key strategies for translators to thrive in 2025
译员在 2025 年蓬勃发展的 5 大关键战略

The translation industry is evolving rapidly, driven by technological advancements, increasing specialization, and the growing demand for high-quality, niche services. To stay ahead, translators must adapt to these changes and refine their practices. Below are five actionable strategies to ensure success in 2025.
在技术进步、专业化程度不断提高以及对高质量利基服务不断增长的需求的推动下，翻译行业正在迅速发展。为了保持领先地位，翻译人员必须适应这些变化并改进他们的实践。以下是确保 2025 年成功的五种可行策略。

1. Leverage AI and CAT Tools
1. 利用 AI 和 CAT 工具

The integration of artificial intelligence (AI) and computer-assisted translation (CAT) tools has revolutionized the translation process. These tools enhance productivity, improve accuracy, and allow translators to handle larger volumes of work more efficiently. AI-powered tools offer advanced suggestions, while CAT tools like SDL Trados, streamline workflows by using translation memories and glossaries.
人工智能 （AI） 和计算机辅助翻译 （CAT） 工具的集成彻底改变了翻译流程。这些工具可以提高生产力、准确性，并使译员能够更高效地处理大量工作。AI 驱动的工具提供高级建议，而 SDL Trados 等 CAT 工具则通过使用翻译记忆库和词汇表来简化工作流程。

Actionable Tip: 可作的提示：
- Stay updated with the latest advancements in AI and CAT technology.

- 随时了解 AI 和 CAT 技术的最新进展。
- Experiment with tools that integrate AI while preserving quality.

- 尝试使用集成 AI 同时保持质量的工具。
- Invest time in learning how to maximize features like termbases, glossaries, and quality assurance checks.

- 投入时间学习如何最大限度地利用术语库、词汇表和质量保证检查等功能。
By effectively leveraging these technologies, translators can offer faster turnaround times without compromising on quality, making them more competitive in a tech-driven market.

通过有效利用这些技术，翻译人员可以在不影响质量的情况下提供更快的周转时间，从而使他们在技术驱动的市场中更具竞争力。
2. Specialize Further

2. 进一步专业化
Generalists face increasing competition in the translation industry, but specialists in fields like legal, medical, technical, and financial translation are in high demand. Specialization not only enhances your credibility but also allows you to charge higher rates. Clients are more likely to trust translators who demonstrate expertise in a specific domain.

通才在翻译行业面临着日益激烈的竞争，但法律、医学、技术和金融翻译等领域的专家需求量很大。专业化不仅可以提高您的可信度，还可以让您收取更高的费用。客户更有可能信任在特定领域展示专业知识的翻译人员。
Actionable Tips: 可行的提示：

- Identify a niche that aligns with your interests or experience.
- 确定与您的兴趣或经验相符的利基市场。

- Pursue certifications or training programs tailored to your chosen field, such as legal translation diplomas or medical terminology courses.
- 追求针对您选择的领域量身定制的认证或培训计划，例如法律翻译文凭或医学术语课程。

- Build a portfolio that highlights your specialized work to attract clients in that sector.
- 建立一个突出您的专业工作的投资组合，以吸引该领域的客户。

A deep understanding of your niche will set you apart and allow you to thrive in a competitive landscape.
深入了解您的利基市场将使您与众不同，并让您在竞争激烈的环境中茁壮成长。

3. 加强在线形象

In today's digital-first world, having a strong online presence is essential for freelancers and agencies. Clients often research potential collaborators online before reaching out, so your digital footprint must showcase your expertise and professionalism. Platforms like LinkedIn, TikTok, Instagram, and personal websites are powerful tools for self-promotion.
在当今数字优先的世界中，拥有强大的在线形象对于自由职业者和代理机构来说至关重要。客户通常会在联系之前在线研究潜在的合作者，因此您的数字足迹必须展示您的专业知识和专业精神。LinkedIn、TikTok、Instagram 和个人网站等平台是自我推销的强大工具。

Actionable Tips: 可行的提示：

Building and maintaining an online presence not only helps you connect with clients but also positions you as a thought leader in the industry.

建立和维护在线形象不仅可以帮助您与客户建立联系，还可以将您定位为该行业的思想领袖。
4. Invest in Continuous Learning

4. 投资于持续学习
The translation industry is constantly evolving, with new fields like localization, subtitling, and post-editing machine translations gaining importance. To remain competitive, translators must commit to lifelong learning by staying updated on trends and acquiring new skills.

翻译行业不断发展，本地化、字幕和译后编辑机器翻译等新领域变得越来越重要。为了保持竞争力，翻译人员必须通过了解最新趋势和获取新技能来致力于终身学习。
Actionable Tips: 可行的提示：

- Enroll in courses on specialized topics, such as video game localization, SEO translation, or machine translation post-editing.
- 报名参加专业主题的课程，例如视频游戏本地化、SEO 翻译或机器翻译译后编辑。

- Attend webinars and conferences to stay informed about industry developments.
- 参加网络研讨会和会议，随时了解行业发展。

- Learn new languages or refine your knowledge of existing ones to expand your market reach.
- 学习新语言或完善现有语言的知识，以扩大您的市场范围。

- Subscribe to industry publications, blogs, and podcasts to stay updated on global trends.
- 订阅行业出版物、博客和播客，随时了解全球趋势。

Continuous learning not only enhances your expertise but also demonstrates to clients that you are committed to professional growth.
持续学习不仅可以提高您的专业知识，还可以向客户展示您致力于专业发展。
