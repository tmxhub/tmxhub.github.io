模特：我的小外甥

网站：https://lmarena.ai/

![用LMArena制作手办风格照片](/img/make-figures-with-LMArena.png){: .shadow}

> 中文提示词：使用nano-banana模型，绘制图中角色的1/7比例的商业化手办，写实风格，真实环境，手办放在电脑桌上，电脑屏幕里的内容为该手办的CAD建模过程，电脑屏幕旁放着印有原画的BANDAI风格的塑料玩具包装盒
生成视频提示词:第一视角，伸出一只手，拿起人物手办，然后拿近慢慢左右旋转，轻轻观察，不露脸。

> 英文提示词：Please turn this photo into a character figure. Behind it, place a box with the character's image printed on it. Next to it, add a computer with its screen showing the Blender modeling process. In front of the box, add a round plastic base for the figure and have it stand on it9, The PVC material of the base should have a crystal-clear, translucent texture, and set the entire scene indoors.
