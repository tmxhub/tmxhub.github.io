### ELEVATE 2025-Motivate

Keynote: Navigating the post-localization landscape: Trends shaping the future of Language Service
主题演讲：驾驭后本地化领域：塑造语言服务未来的趋势

This keynote session examines key trends transforming the language industry as it evolves in the post-localization era. Topics include advancements in accessibility, the growing role of AI, and strategies for achieving measurable ROI. Attendees will explore how to align localization with accessibility and user experience while addressing emerging challenges.
本次主题演讲探讨了在后本地化时代发展过程中改变语言行业的主要趋势。主题包括可访问性的进步、AI 的日益重要作用以及实现可衡量的 ROI 的策略。与会者将探索如何在应对新出现的挑战的同时，使本地化与可访问性和用户体验保持一致。

The session also provides practical approaches to leveraging AI and data-driven solutions, offering a clear perspective to help organizations adapt and succeed in a rapidly changing global landscape.
该会议还提供了利用 AI 和数据驱动型解决方案的实用方法，为帮助组织在快速变化的全球环境中适应并取得成功提供了清晰的视角。

The future localization platform: Translating everything in 2030
未来的本地化平台：在 2030 年翻译一切

Over the past five years, technological advancements have reshaped the way we work at an unprecedented pace. But what about the next five years? Will innovation continue at the same breakneck speed, and how will it shape the future of localization?
在过去的五年里，技术进步以前所未有的速度重塑了我们的工作方式。但未来五年呢？创新是否会以同样惊人的速度继续下去，它将如何塑造本地化的未来？

In this session, we’ll explore what localization might look like in 2030, examining emerging trends and potential disruptions. We’ll also discuss the rise of agentic AI—intelligent systems that take on more autonomous roles in workflows, improving decision-making, automating processes, and enhancing consistency across languages. How will these advancements transform the industry, and what opportunities and challenges will they bring?
在本次会议中，我们将探讨 2030 年的本地化可能是什么样子，研究新兴趋势和潜在的颠覆性因素。我们还将讨论代理 AI 的兴起，代理 AI 是一种智能系统，在工作流程中承担更多自主角色，可改进决策、自动化流程并增强语言间的一致性。这些进步将如何改变行业，它们将带来哪些机遇和挑战？

Why cloud and AI are the tech power duo
为什么云和 AI 是技术强者

As technology continues to evolve, the combination of cloud and AI is transforming how businesses operate and scale. In this session, presented by AWS, we’ll explore how cloud technology and AI work seamlessly together to drive efficiency, innovation, and scalability across industries. From enhancing decision-making processes to streamlining workflows, this powerful duo is revolutionizing how businesses manage and leverage data. Join AWS as they dive into the practical applications of cloud and AI, and discover how this dynamic partnership is shaping the future of technology.
随着技术的不断发展，云和 AI 的结合正在改变企业的运营和扩展方式。在本次由 AWS 主讲的会议中，我们将探讨云技术和 AI 如何无缝协作，以推动各行各业的效率、创新和可扩展性。从增强决策流程到简化工作流程，这对强大的二人组正在彻底改变企业管理和利用数据的方式。加入 AWS，他们将深入研究云和 AI 的实际应用，并探索这种充满活力的合作伙伴关系如何塑造技术的未来。

Re-imagining corporate translations in the era of AI
在 AI 时代重新构想企业翻译

The Dell team will walk through the process we followed as we re-imagined translations at Dell through our migration from traditional translation practices used via TMS to the Evolve based processes enabled via Trados Enterprise.
Dell 团队将介绍我们在 Dell 重新构想翻译时遵循的流程，从通过 TMS 使用的传统翻译实践迁移到通过 Trados Enterprise 启用的基于 Evolve 的流程。

How can you safeguard privacy today and in the future?
您如何保护当今和未来的隐私？

As AI continues to transform the localization industry, the handling of sensitive linguistic data has never been more critical. With increasing concerns over data privacy, security, and compliance, organizations and individuals must navigate a complex landscape of regulations while leveraging AI-driven technologies to streamline workflows.
随着 AI 不断改变本地化行业，敏感语言数据的处理从未像现在这样重要。随着对数据隐私、安全性和合规性的日益关注，组织和个人必须在复杂的法规环境中导航，同时利用 AI 驱动的技术来简化工作流程。

In this panel, we’ll explore the key data privacy challenges facing the industry, from protecting confidential information to ensuring compliance with evolving regulations like GDPR and the AI Act. Our speakers will share best practices for mitigating risks, securing AI-powered localization processes, and striking the right balance between innovation and data protection.
在本小组讨论中，我们将探讨该行业面临的关键数据隐私挑战，从保护机密信息到确保遵守 GDPR 和 AI 法案等不断发展的法规。我们的演讲者将分享降低风险、保护 AI 驱动的本地化流程以及在创新和数据保护之间取得适当平衡的最佳实践。

Where accessibility and localization converge
可访问性和本地化的交汇点

As global businesses strive to reach diverse audiences, accessibility and localization are increasingly interconnected. With new accessibility regulations coming into effect, organizations must rethink how they deliver content that is both linguistically and functionally inclusive.
随着全球企业努力接触不同的受众，可访问性和本地化的联系越来越紧密。随着新的辅助功能法规的生效，组织必须重新考虑如何提供在语言和功能上具有包容性的内容。

We’ll explore how accessibility and localization are two sides of the same coin—facing similar challenges, requiring shared expertise, and ultimately working toward the same goal: making content truly usable for all. Including the impact of evolving laws, the synergies between these disciplines, and practical guidance for integrating accessibility into your localization practices.
我们将探讨可访问性和本地化如何成为同一枚硬币的两面 - 面临类似的挑战，需要共享专业知识，并最终朝着同一个目标努力：让内容真正为所有人所用。包括不断发展的法律的影响、这些学科之间的协同作用，以及将辅助功能整合到本地化实践中的实用指南。

当今翻译中的道德困境以及如何克服这些困境

As the localization industry evolves, so do the ethical challenges facing translation professionals. 随着本地化行业的发展，翻译专业人员面临的道德挑战也在不断增加。从 AI 生成的内容和机器翻译偏见到文化敏感性和数据隐私问题，译员和本地化团队必须在保持准确性、公平性和完整性的同时应对复杂的道德困境。

What responsibilities do localization professionals have in preventing misinformation or cultural misrepresentation? And how can businesses balance speed, cost, and quality without compromising ethical principles?

从个人翻译人员到大型企业，本次会议将提供有关如何使您的 AI 投资与您的独特需求保持一致的可行见解，而不会被最新趋势所困扰。避免追逐新闻，专注于为您的运营带来最大价值的事情，确保您的 AI 工具不仅是尖端的，而且对您的工作流程真正有效。

Through the lawyer's lens: Legal translation in the AI revolution
通过律师的视角：AI 革命中的法律翻译

In the fast-evolving era of legal technology, the integration of AI into legal translation is revolutionizing how lawyers and translators manage the accuracy and consistency of language across documents. This talk explores how AI tools can streamline the use of terminology by referencing multiple authoritative resources, such as statute books, preferred judicial terms, layman language, and relevant case law.
在法律技术快速发展的时代，将 AI 集成到法律翻译中正在彻底改变律师和翻译人员管理跨文档语言的准确性和一致性的方式。本次讲座探讨了 AI 工具如何通过引用多种权威资源（例如成文法书籍、首选司法术语、外行语言和相关判例法）来简化术语的使用。

While AI can handle the technical task of harmonizing terminology, human expertise remains essential for refining sentence flow and maintaining readability. By tailoring language style to the preferences of individual lawyers or businesses, AI-assisted translation achieves a balance between legal precision and accessibility, meeting the needs of diverse audiences.
虽然 AI 可以处理协调术语的技术任务，但人类专业知识对于改进句子流程和保持可读性仍然至关重要。通过根据律师个人或企业的偏好定制语言风格，AI 辅助翻译在法律准确性和可访问性之间实现了平衡，满足了不同受众的需求。

This session will provide practical insights into how AI tools are shaping the future of legal translation, empowering legal professionals to produce documents that uphold the highest standards of linguistic and legal integrity.
本次会议将提供有关 AI 工具如何塑造法律翻译的未来，使法律专业人士能够制作维护最高语言和法律诚信标准的文档的实用见解。

Organizational growth framework: Integrating localization as your company scales
组织发展框架：随着公司规模的扩大而整合本地化

Recognizing these early signs helps guide the transition to a centralized and efficient localization model.
在全球化的商业环境中，战略性地整合本地化计划对于可扩展性和成功至关重要。本地化工作通常从初始指标开始，这些指标表明需要结构化方法。组织可能会注意到翻译不一致、重复工作或难以为全球受众扩展内容等挑战。识别这些早期迹象有助于指导过渡到集中高效的本地化模式。

In this session we’ll introduce an "Organizational Growth Framework" outlining how localization evolves with a company's development. This framework provides a roadmap for aligning localization with organizational maturity, ensuring it supports growth and global ambitions.
在本次会议中，我们将介绍一个“组织成长框架”，概述本地化如何随着公司的发展而发展。该框架为使本地化与组织成熟度保持一致提供了路线图，确保它支持增长和全球雄心壮志。
